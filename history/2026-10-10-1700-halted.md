## Public-node test, 14:23 to 16:18 CEST (12:23 to 14:18 UTC)

Ordered by stp at 14:20 CEST: as many bot senders as the box can run, on the five public TN10 nodes at random, for about two hours. Report and data: private repo `tn10-storm-1010-public-nodes`.

- **Peak (Claim, measured on TN10):** about 3,170 bot tx/s submitted, about 2,970 accepted, at 14:38 CEST (22 senders). Earlier about 2,430 tx/s on 19 senders.
- **Clean hold (Claim):** about 880 to 985 bot tx/s on 14 senders, zero rejects, to 15:57 CEST.
- **Fees:** 200, then 400 sompi/gram from 14:26 CEST (stp: "scale and use double fees").
- **Build** sent to the same five nodes from about 14:22 CEST, so network counts in that window are combined.
- **What broke (Claim):** the launcher ran twice and senders piled past 20; the rate fell from about 2,850 to about 200 tx/s; the out-of-memory killer took n2 at about 14:51 CEST. The coin list ran to 0 unclaimed at about 15:10 CEST (250,000 tKAS top-up at 15:13). n2's tip froze at about 15:57 CEST; restarted 16:19, healthy about 16:21. boson, electron and neutrino often refused connections when many senders booted together (**Not sure / open for debate** why).
- The senders ran past the planned 16:10 CEST stop and were killed at 16:18.

## Learned

- A run with two sides needs an explicit "Build started?" check before T0.
- A reader node must not sit in the submit path, and its lag must not zero the senders.
- One locked scheduler, a fixed sender count, staggered launches and a relaunch limit; a halt stops the watchdog first.
- Count from the senders' per-second files.

These are built into the [bot run and build run plan](../go/README.md), T0 20:30 CEST.
